# Community conduct

TinyOffice aims to maintain a focused, respectful, and technically honest project community.

Participants are expected to:

- discuss ideas and tradeoffs without personal attacks;
- give specific, actionable feedback;
- respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's data or credentials;
- disclose conflicts of interest and security concerns responsibly;
- accept project-maintainer decisions while remaining free to fork under the project license.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, and doxxing are not acceptable. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close participation, or ban accounts when needed to protect the community.
